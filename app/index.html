Pass rush & pass protection

Football up front is won in pairs and trios.

An edge rusher gets home because his partner draws the double team. A guard holds because his center helps. Trench Partners measures which teammates actually win together, using every charted pass play from the first eight weeks of 2021.

Three tools

How a match is scored

Chart every snap

On each of 8,557 pass plays, PFF scouts recorded who rushed, who blocked, and who caused or allowed a hurry, hit or sack.

Group teammates

For every snap we list each pair and trio of teammates who rushed together, or blocked together, on that play.

Count wins

Rushers win when the defense pressures the QB. Blockers win when the pocket stays clean. We count wins over all their shared snaps.

Rank fairly

A group needs 30+ snaps together. Small samples are pulled toward the league average, so a lucky handful of plays can't top the list.

Words you'll see

Pressure rate
Share of pass rushes where the defense got a hurry, hit or sack. League average: .
Clean pocket rate
Share of protection snaps with no hurry, hit or sack allowed. League average: .
Together vs apart
The group's rate on shared snaps, compared with the same players on snaps without each other. "+12 pts vs apart" means they are 12 points better together.
Percentile
Where a player ranks among everyone at his role with 50+ snaps. 90th means better than 90% of them.
Alignment
Where a player lined up before the snap: LT/LG/C/RG/RT on the line, LEO/REO for wide edge rushers, DLT/DRT for interior tackles, NT over the center, OLB/ILB for linebackers.
Who made the play
On shared snaps that ended in pressure, which member of the group was credited. For blockers, who gave it up.

Data: NFL Big Data Bowl 2023 (Next Gen Stats tracking and PFF scouting, 2021 weeks 1–8). Headshots and draft details: nflverse public roster.
